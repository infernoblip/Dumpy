Give away or sell your extra stuff to neighbours, and pick up things you need, right inside your housing society.

How Dumpy works

  • Free Tap I want this and the owner picks who gets it.
  • ₹ Paid items are cheap. You pay the owner directly by UPI or cash. Dumpy never handles money.
  • Delivery: the owner brings it to your flat, you collect it, or a neighbour helper brings it for a tip you both agree.
