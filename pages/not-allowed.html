Not allowed on Dumpy

Dumpy is for everyday things neighbours can safely pass on. Please don't post:

  • Medicines, including leftover tablets, syrups and injections
  • Alcohol
  • Tobacco, cigarettes, vapes and gutka
  • Weapons, including knives sold as weapons, air guns and fireworks
  • Expired food, or any opened or home-cooked food
  • Anything illegal to sell, or that isn't yours to give

If you see one of these, open the item and tap Report this item. Your society admin can take it down, and can suspend anyone who keeps posting them.
