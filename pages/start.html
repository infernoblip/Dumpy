Start Dumpy in your society

Bring Dumpy to your own housing society. Your neighbours get their own private space: nobody outside your society sees your items or who you are.

  1. Sign in with your email. No password: we email you a link.
  2. Tell us your society's name and city. The Dumpy team checks each new society, usually within a day.
  3. You become its admin. You get an invite code to share with neighbours, and you approve each person who joins.
